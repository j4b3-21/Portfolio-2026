## What I'm exploring

A different model of computation itself — information held in superposition, transformed by gates, limited by decoherence. Not the press-release version; the version where you can reason about why Shor's algorithm is fast and why a fault-tolerant machine is still hard.

## Why it interests me

Understanding the foundations is the whole game here — there is no improvising around the linear algebra. And there is a second stake: the Quantum Club (co-founded 26 October 2026) makes this exploration pedagogical — learning it well enough to hand it to the next person whole.

## Concepts I'm studying

Qubits and superposition; entanglement as a resource; quantum gates and circuits; decoherence and error correction; the shape of quantum algorithms versus classical ones.
