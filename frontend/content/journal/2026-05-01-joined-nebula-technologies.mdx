In May 2026, I joined **Nebula Technologies Labs Pvt. Ltd.** as a Founding Software Engineer Intern.

The work combined product engineering and infrastructure: I helped architect and develop a production CRM platform with Java, Spring Boot, React, PostgreSQL and REST APIs, while also designing its Docker, Nginx, VPS and Cloudflare setup.

I built Jenkins and GitHub Actions pipelines that reduced deployment time from approximately 10 minutes to under 1 minute. I also worked on backend testing and debugging, PostgreSQL indexing and query optimization, transactions, migrations, JSONB, connection pooling, and concurrent and asynchronous components using Java concurrency, RabbitMQ and Kafka.

The role ran from May through August 2026, and involved working with a six-member engineering team across technical design, code reviews, issue tracking and production releases.
