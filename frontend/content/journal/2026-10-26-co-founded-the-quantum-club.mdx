With a classmate, I co-founded the **first Quantum Club at IIIT Tiruchirappalli** — and I'm now its head.

## Milestones

- **26 October 2026 — Founded.** The first quantum computing community at IIIT Tiruchirappalli becomes official.

## Why

Quantum computing had no campus home — the club is the room where a beginner can ask "what is a qubit, really?" without feeling behind.

## Vision

A small, serious community that learns in public: shared notes so nobody starts from scratch alone.

## Activities — planned

- Workshops taking members from zero to a first quantum circuit.
- Reading groups that don't skip the math.
- Shared notes and public write-ups.
